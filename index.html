Plan Your Visit

Everything you need before you come

🛂 Visa

Many countries get Visa on Arrival. KAZA UniVisa (Zimbabwe + Zambia) costs $50 USD cash. Bring passport valid 2 months.

💵 Money

Prices are in USD. Bring small notes ($1, $5, $10). Community tours are cash / EcoCash. ATMs can be unreliable.

🚐 Transport

Matobo tour includes transport. Khami - $30 return. We arrange pick-up from your hotel in Bulawayo.

🛡️ Safety

Bulawayo tourist areas are generally safe. Travel with a local guide, keep valuables safe, respect local culture.
